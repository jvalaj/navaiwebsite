How it works
/001 Understanding

Understanding what’s happening on your screen

Nav continuously interprets what's visible on your screen using a multimodal system that combines visual understanding with language context. It recognizes interface elements, text, layouts, and state—so it understands not just what an app is, but what it's currently showing.

/002 Planning

Deciding the next steps like a user would

Based on your request and what it sees on screen, Nav plans a sequence of actions to reach the desired outcome. It reasons through each step, adapting if the interface changes—similar to how a person would navigate menus, dialogs, and workflows.

/003 Action

Taking actions and learning from the results

Nav performs actions directly on your Mac—clicking, typing, searching, and navigating apps—while observing the results of each step. It maintains short-term context and memory so it can stay oriented, recover from errors, and continue tasks without starting over.

Understanding Nav

What is Nav? ×
Nav is an on-device AI agent that can navigate apps, understand what's on your screen, and perform actions like clicking, typing, and searching—just like a user would.
How does Nav work? +
Nav uses a multimodal AI system that sees your screen, understands the current context, plans the right actions, and executes them natively on your Mac—just like a human user would.
What kinds of tasks can Nav handle? +
Nav can handle a wide range of tasks including navigating apps, filling out forms, managing files, switching between windows, and completing multi-step workflows across different applications.
Does Nav work with all apps? +
Nav supports 50+ popular macOS applications and is continuously expanding its compatibility. It works with most standard macOS apps that follow Apple's interface guidelines.
Is my data private? +
Yes. Nav runs locally on your Mac. Your screen data and actions stay on your device. You remain in full control at all times.
